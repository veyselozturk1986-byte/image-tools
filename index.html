🛡️ Zero-Server Policy: How to Verify?

PrivacyConvert operates entirely inside your browser sandbox using client-side JavaScript, WebAssembly, and local APIs. No files, passwords, or texts ever leave your device or get uploaded to any remote server.

🔍 How can you verify this yourself?

  1. Open your browser's Developer Tools by pressing F12 or right-clicking and selecting Inspect.
  2. Go to the **Network** tab.
  3. Process any PDF or image file. Notice that **zero outgoing data transfer or file upload requests** are made to external servers.
  4. You can even disconnect your internet entirely; the tools will continue working offline!
