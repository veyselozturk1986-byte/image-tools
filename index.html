Frequently Asked Questions & Security Architecture

How does PrivacyConvert process files without a server?

PrivacyConvert uses advanced client-side browser technologies (such as WebAssembly, PDF.js, and HTML5 Canvas). When you select or drop a file, your browser's own CPU and memory handle the conversion locally.

Do my files ever leave my device?

Never. Because there is no backend server processing your files, your sensitive documents, PDFs, and images never touch the internet or any remote cloud storage. They remain 100% on your local machine.

Why is this approach safer than traditional online converters?

Traditional online converters require you to upload your sensitive files to their remote servers, posing major data breach and privacy risks. PrivacyConvert eliminates this risk entirely by keeping data handling strictly offline-capable within your secure browser sandbox.
