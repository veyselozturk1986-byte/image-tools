Why Convert HEIC Photos Locally?

iPhones save photos in HEIC format to save storage, but Windows and web applications often can't open them. Uploading personal family or document photos to online servers creates privacy risks. With PrivacyConvert, the conversion happens entirely within your device's memory.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is HEIC and why can't I open it on Windows?

HEIC (High Efficiency Image Container) is Apple's default image format. Windows PCs require native app extensions or conversion to standard JPG format to view them.

Are my private photos uploaded anywhere?

Absolutely not. Your photos are decoded and converted locally in your web browser using HTML5 Canvas and Javascript.
